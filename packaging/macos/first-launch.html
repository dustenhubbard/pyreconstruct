@APP_NAME@

Open @APP_NAME@ for the first time

Do these steps in order. Keep @APP_NAME@ closed until step 3.

1

Copy the app

Drag @APP_NAME@ onto the Applications folder in the installer window.

Wait for the copy to finish.

2

Run one command in Terminal

Hold Command and press the space bar. Type Terminal, then press Return.

Copy this command. Click Copy command, or click the line and hold Command and press C.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/@APP_NAME@.app"

Click inside the Terminal window. Hold Command and press V to paste. Press Return.

A new line with no message means it worked. You can close Terminal.

3

Open the app

Open the Applications folder and double-click @APP_NAME@.

Next time, open it from Applications the normal way.

Did Terminal show a message?

Do not open the app yet. Find the message or problem below.

No such file or directory

@APP_NAME@ is not in the Applications folder. The copy did not finish, or the app went to the Trash.

  1. Drag @APP_NAME@ from the installer window onto Applications again.
  2. Wait for the copy to finish.
  3. Do step 2 again.

I opened the app too early, or it is stuck

  1. If macOS shows this box, click Done. Do not click Move to Trash.
  2. If the app is still open, hold Command and Option and press Esc. Select @APP_NAME@, then click Force Quit.
  3. Do step 2 again, then step 3.
