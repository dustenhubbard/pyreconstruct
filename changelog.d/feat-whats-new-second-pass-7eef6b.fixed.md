- **In the dark theme, buttons in dialogs are no longer tight boxes around their
  words.** The dark style gave every button 2 pixels of padding, so `Close` in
  `Help > View log file...` was 40 pixels wide against 64 in the light theme.
  Buttons in dialogs now have 12 pixels on each side; OK and Cancel rows, and
  the buttons in the main window, are unchanged.
