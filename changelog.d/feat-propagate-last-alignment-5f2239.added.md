- **You can align a section first and propagate the alignment afterward.**
  Alignments > Propagate transform > Start propagation recording now picks up
  every transform change you have made on the current section since you last
  changed the transform of a different section, so an Align by correlation you
  have already checked can go straight to Propagate to start or Propagate to
  end without being done again. It starts over after you record, undo or redo a
  transform change, switch alignments, or make a series-wide change such as
  importing transforms, a series undo, or inserting or reordering sections.
