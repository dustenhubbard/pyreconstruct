- **The scale bar is 15 pixels thick by default.** A thickness already saved
  is kept.
- **The Scale Bar group in `Series > Options... > View` is easier to read.** The
  fixed length field is enabled only in fixed length mode, the size slider says
  when it is the most the bar can grow, the note about stepping by tens is one
  line under the length, and thickness comes last. Every control previews on
  the bar, not only the two sliders.
