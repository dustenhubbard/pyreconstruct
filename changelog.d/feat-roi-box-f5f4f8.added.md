- **An ROI box shows only the traces in one area of the field.** Right-click the
  field and choose `View` > `Toggle ROI box`, or press B. Enter the box's width
  and height in μm (5 x 5 the first time), then click where the box goes. Only
  traces that touch the box are drawn, and only they can be clicked, lassoed,
  selected with Select all, or selected by Find. A trace is drawn in full, even
  where it reaches past the box. The box keeps its place when you page through
  sections, so you can check one area on every section. Click again to move
  the box; any other tool works as usual inside it. `View` > `Move ROI box` goes
  back to moving it, and `View` > `ROI box size...` changes its size. The status
  bar shows the box and its size while it is on. The box changes only what you
  see: nothing is saved, Undo still works, and the box adds no Undo step. Toggle
  it again to see every trace.
