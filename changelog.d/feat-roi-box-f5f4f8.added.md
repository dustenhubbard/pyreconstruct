- **An ROI box shows only the traces whose bounds touch one area of the
  field.** Right-click the field and choose `View` > `Toggle ROI box`, or press
  `B`. Enter a width and height in μm, then click to place the box. The box
  keeps its place across sections and saves nothing. While it is on, a field
  command that acts on every trace of an object asks you to turn the box off
  first.
