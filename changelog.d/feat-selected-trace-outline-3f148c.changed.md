- **Selected traces stand out on any image and in any color.** A selected trace
  is now drawn with a black edge and a white band on either side of its line,
  with its own color down the middle. The old highlight was a faint, wider
  stroke in the trace's own color, which was easy to miss for a light trace on
  a light image or a dark trace on a dark one. The outline is also drawn over
  a trace's fill, so a filled trace shows its selection too.
