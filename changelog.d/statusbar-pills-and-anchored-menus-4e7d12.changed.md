- **The status bar's clickable segments look like buttons, and their menus
  open where you clicked.** Each segment draws as a small rounded button that
  fills on hover. Clicking one opens its menu just above the segment:
  alignments and brightness/contrast profiles present the same way, with the
  current one checked and highlighted. The section segment opens a slim
  scrollable list of every section with a jump field on top: type a number
  and press Enter, from anywhere in the series. Right-clicking it opens the
  classic Go To Section dialog.
