- **`Series ▸ Clean up ▸ Duplicates...` replaces `Remove duplicate traces...`
  and `Find duplicates named differently...`.** One scan finds overlapping
  traces whether their names match or not, and lists each structure once,
  however many times it was traced. Each row has a drop-down of its names.
  Combining a row keeps one trace under the name picked, adds the tags of the
  other traces to it, and deletes the rest. Nothing changes until you combine,
  a locked object never loses a trace, and each combine is one undo.
