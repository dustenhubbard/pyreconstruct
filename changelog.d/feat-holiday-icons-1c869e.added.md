- **The app icon dresses up for the holidays.** From two weeks before until a
  week after New Year's Day, Valentine's Day, St. Patrick's Day, Easter,
  Independence Day, Halloween and Christmas, the window and taskbar show a
  themed version of the icon: the cells take the holiday's colors, with a small
  touch such as string lights and snow, a spider web or hearts. On macOS the
  dock icon changes too while PyReconstruct is open. PyReconstruct Dev keeps
  its DEV badge. Overlapping weeks go to the nearer holiday, the choice is made
  at launch, and every other day the icon is unchanged. The dated welcome
  images still appear as before.
