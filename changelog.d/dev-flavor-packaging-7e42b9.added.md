- **PyReconstruct Dev installs beside stable.** The test build is its own
  application: its own name, its own dark icon, its own installer identity on
  Windows and macOS, and its own settings, so nothing it stores can touch the
  stable app's state. It follows the beta update channel; stable follows
  stable releases. One series still opens in only one app at a time, and the
  in-use message says which one has it.
