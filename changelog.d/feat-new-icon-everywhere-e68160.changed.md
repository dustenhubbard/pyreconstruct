- **PyReconstruct has a new icon.** The Python-snakes mark is replaced by a
  stack of three glass sections: raw EM at the bottom, a few traced cells in
  the middle and fully segmented cells on top. It is the app icon in the
  window, taskbar, dock and installers, with simpler artwork at the small sizes
  so it stays clear in a title bar. PyReconstruct Dev uses the same icon with
  an amber DEV badge, which becomes an amber dot at the smallest sizes. The
  pyreconstruct.org site uses the stack on its own as its logo and the app
  icon as its favicon.
